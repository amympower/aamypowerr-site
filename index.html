She builds brands from scratch for athletes, creators, serial founders, and A-list talent — leading positioning, narrative, creative, and go-to-market from day one. Her specialty is food, beverage, CPG, restaurants, and hospitality: industries where brand lives in the physical world and marketing has to perform under pressure.

Over the past decade she's led creative and growth work at R/GA, founded and scaled the agency Vocabulary from two people to fifteen, and steered This Place through a merger and sale — working alongside clients like PepsiCo, Nike, Spotify, Verizon, and Scott Galloway along the way.
